About Scopelab

Committed to comprehensive healthcare solutions since 2016.

Scopelab Healthcare Ltd is one of Kenya's fastest-growing suppliers of medical, dental, theatre and laboratory equipment, built by a team of skilled and determined professionals dedicated to cost-effective, modern healthcare technology.

We're a duly registered private company, fully compliant with Kenya's tax regulatory bodies. Operating in isolation has limited room for success, so we partner with vibrant, reliable manufacturers and distributors both within and outside Kenya to keep our clients equipped, timely, and ahead.

  • Registered, tax-compliant Kenyan company
  • Trusted regional & international manufacturing partners
  • Serving hospitals, clinics & laboratories nationwide
